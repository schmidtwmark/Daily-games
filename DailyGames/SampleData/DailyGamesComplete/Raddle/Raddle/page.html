Clues, in order

Move the 2nd letter of PALE to the end to get a request → PLEA
PLEA DEAL, something discussed in court
Swap the first and last letters of DEAL to be in charge → LEAD
Symbol for LEAD → PB
PB & J
What airline follows J? → KLM
Reverse KLM and insert an I to get a liquid → MILK
MILK & HONEY, a term of abundance
HONEY is stored in a POT (at least in the emoji)
A greener term for POT → GRASS
GRASSHOPPER, an insect

About this theme

Raddle is a year old! To celebrate, here are five days of Raddles about paper, the traditional first anniversary gift. Each Raddle goes from "pa-" to "-per". Thanks for supporting this game, it's been so fun to make over the last year. Here's to many more!
